⇄ BioCrossroads-specific decisions vs Ascend baseline
  • Palette: BioCrossroads navy #001442, royal blue #003DCC and cyan #66D4FF (Ascend baseline uses Charcoal #1C1C1C + Ascend Blue #45AADD). Values read from the live site.
  • Scope: Life sciences focus. Copy and categories emphasize biosciences, medical devices, pharma, diagnostics and agbiosciences. Ascend's platform spans all industries.
  • Typography: Headlines in Roboto, as on their site. Body on their site is Sofia Pro, licensed through their Adobe Fonts kit. Figtree stands in here until BioCrossroads confirms we can use their kit.
  • Shape: Pill-shaped buttons (their site) instead of the squarer Conexus buttons.
  • Co-branding: "Powered by The Ascend Network" credited in the footer and sign-up hero, same as Conexus.
